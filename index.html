NATIONAL RESEARCH / DIFFERENT DATA UNIVERSES

U.S. & Texas evidence

The existing national research is preserved inside this workspace. FBI-reported homicide offenses and CDC death-certificate deaths answer different questions; figures are never merged.

!
Coverage matters. FBI 2021 agency transition left national reporting below the surrounding years; a dip in published police totals that year is not an established decline. CDC and police measures use different reporting systems.

!
