What must be built before selling licenses

1 · Accounts and sign-in

Secure user authentication, invitations, tenant membership, and recovery flows.

2 · Enforce subscriptions server-side

Signed payment webhooks, entitlement checks, trial expiration, renewals, failed payments, and seat limits.

3 · Protect research workspaces

End-to-end organization isolation, row-level security policies, review permissions and audit logs.

4 · Commercial readiness

Evaluate source-data licensing terms, accuracy disclaimers, privacy and retention practices, pricing, support, accessibility and billing operations.

Suggested business model: open public evidence and methodology, paid governed team workflows, and institution-specific service agreements. Pricing is intentionally not set before customer interviews and data rights review.
