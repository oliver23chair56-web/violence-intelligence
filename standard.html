VIRS is the proposed Violence Intelligence Reporting Standard. It specifies the units, evidence rules, source lineage, missingness states, and revision history needed to combine city gun-violence records responsibly. It is an independent research proposal, not an adopted governmental standard.

Defined fields—Typed and source-qualified
Chicago pilot groups—Provisional police case groupings
Linked victim rows—Source-record links
Approved motive claims—No automatic attribution
Important distinction: A police case number does not automatically equal one independently confirmed shooting event. The 500 Chicago pilot groupings are provisional. Their 576 linked victimization rows are sourced; their motive and interpersonal circumstances remain unreviewed.

Three evidence layers

LAYER 1

Recorded facts

Occurrence time, official source identifiers, documented firearm or gunshot involvement, victimizations, fatalities, and amendments. Preserve the original source's unit of analysis.

LAYER 2

Documented circumstances

Reported preceding dispute, relationship, gang context, planning, or possible objective—each independently attributable to evidence and open to contradiction.

LAYER 3

Research interpretations

Expressive, instrumental, mixed, or unresolved interpretations only after human review. No motive is inferred from neighborhood, demographics, offense codes, or hour of day.

The universal record structure

Source → Provisional event group → Offenses / Victimizations

Event group → Attributed evidence claims → Reviews / revisions

Victim outcomes and the time of a later death may change. Every correction should be traceable rather than silently overwriting prior interpretations.

A single event may link to several offense entries and several affected people. A source identifier is never used alone as proof of event boundaries.

Evidence states—not forced answers

Not assessed: no classification attempted.

Unknown: reviewed, but evidence insufficient.

Documented: sourced claim; independently corroborated if separate support exists.

Disputed: credible conflicting accounts.

Confirmed absent: affirmative evidence supports nonoccurrence, not merely lack of evidence.

Initial quality gates

A pilot import cannot be considered trustworthy until it passes basic consistency tests. These live checks cover the pilot's event/victim linkage; they do not replace human case review.

No incident-level motive claims are published from this schema. Approved interpretations require evidence and a reviewer.

Mapping cities into VIRS

Chicago currently supports victim-level gunshot counts. Baltimore contains a source shooting flag, Kansas City a firearm-used flag, and Detroit/Oakland broader offense categories. The last three signals do not establish gunshot injuries.

The city source retains its original codes. VIRS adds a typed mapping and limitations without silently converting one unit into another.

Next validation work

Human review must confirm case grouping, positive/negative firearm evidence, source completeness, and contradiction handling. The next cross-city test should add independently verified shooting-event or gunshot-victim fields from additional sources, not treat selected offense entries as equivalent events.
